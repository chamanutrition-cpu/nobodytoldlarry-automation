Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

"NTL Poster" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Nobody Told Larry channels to publish the owner's own videos and read the owner's own channel statistics on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram.

What data the tool accesses

  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read the channel's public video statistics (views, likes, comments).
  • Facebook and Instagram: permission to publish reels to the owner's Page and account and to read their post insights.

How the data is used

The data is used only to post the owner's videos on schedule and to keep a private performance log for the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never published. Aggregate post statistics are stored in the owner's repository.

Your choices

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account (Security → Third-party access) or Facebook settings (Business integrations). Revoking access stops the tool immediately.

YouTube API use is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions: reach the Nobody Told Larry team through the channel's YouTube page.
